## 4. Abstract Requirements

According to the official ICIF Abstract Template:

### Formatting

- Abstract must be a concise summary of the presentation.
- Full abstract is limited to **1 page**.
- Body text:
  - Times New Roman
  - 12-point
  - Single-spaced
- References may be included using the format demonstrated in the template.
- Final abstract must be saved as **PDF**.
- PDF is uploaded through the submission portal.

### Author information

The template requires:

- Abstract title
- Author names
- Author affiliations
- Full affiliation address
- Corresponding/presenting author indication
- Email

The presenting author's name must be **bold and underlined**.

Affiliations must be:

- Times New Roman
- 10-point
- Centered
- Italicized

### Important observation

The template does **not specify a numerical word limit**. The stated constraint is that the complete abstract must fit within **one page**.

## 6. Peer Review

ICIF states that each submitted full paper will be evaluated by at least two independent reviewers.

Review criteria include:

- Originality and novelty
- Scientific and technical contribution
- Methodological soundness
- Quality of analysis and results
- Relevance to conference scope
- Clarity and organization
- References and scholarly context
- Ethical and research-integrity compliance

Possible decisions listed:

- Accept
- Accept with Revision
- Revise and Resubmit
- Reject

## 7. Publication

ICIF states that accepted papers are intended for publication in:

**Novel Future Proceedings**

with:

- ISBN
- DOI

ICIF states that selected quality papers will be considered for Scopus indexing.

Scopus indexing should therefore NOT be treated as guaranteed for every accepted paper.

## 8. Research Integrity Rules

ICIF requires original, unpublished work and prohibits:

- Plagiarism
- Self-plagiarism
- Duplicate/simultaneous submission
- Fabrication
- Falsification
- Manipulation of results
- Unethical research practices
- Copyright infringement
- Authorship misconduct
- Peer-review manipulation

Submitted papers may undergo similarity/originality checks.

---

## 9. Generative AI Policy

ICIF permits limited generative-AI assistance for purposes such as:

- Language improvement
- Grammar correction
- Formatting assistance

Authors remain responsible for:

- Accuracy
- Originality
- Integrity
- Scholarly content
- References
- Citations
- Technical claims

Generative AI must not be used to fabricate:

- Data
- Results
- References
- Experimental findings
- Research evidence

Any required AI disclosure must follow the applicable publication policy.
